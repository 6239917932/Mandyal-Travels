# AI support and administration governance

AI may summarize approved portal data, suggest search filters, draft support replies, and prioritize administrative work, but it must not invent availability, price, policy, refund eligibility, identity status, or payment outcomes. Transactional facts always come from deterministic services and live governed records.

Production AI requires provider approval, data-minimization, prompt-injection defenses, output schemas, citations to internal records where appropriate, human confirmation for consequential actions, audit logs, latency/cost limits, abuse monitoring, evaluation sets, fallback behavior, and a kill switch. Secrets and unrestricted personal data must never enter prompts.
