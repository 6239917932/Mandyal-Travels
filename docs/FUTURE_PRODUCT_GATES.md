# Future product gates

Trains, visas, insurance, activities, and transfers are registered as planned modules rather than fake live inventory. Each must reuse the platform's identity, consent, quote, payment, notification, audit, servicing, finance, risk, and API conventions.

No module may become active until its blueprint, legal/commercial ownership, provider contract, normalized domain model, availability/price source, booking and cancellation lifecycle, customer support process, finance/reconciliation, privacy/security review, accessibility/mobile behavior, automated tests, observability, pilot, and rollback are complete. Product-specific regulatory approval is mandatory for visas and insurance.
