# Legal compliance and management approval record

## Purpose

This record documents management approval and the remaining compliance decisions for the hotel-first launch of Mandyal Travels Services Private Limited. Cars, buses, flights, loyalty, wallet funding, external OTA synchronization, statutory GST invoicing, live payments, and automated supplier payouts remain disabled or controlled by separate launch gates.

External legal advice may be obtained when management considers it necessary, but it is not represented as a statutory launch certificate. Management remains responsible for compliance and for preserving the approved versions and launch evidence.

## Business model for review

- Website operator: Mandyal Travels Services Private Limited.
- Launch scope: hotel discovery, hotel booking facilitation, hotel partner onboarding, and hotel PMS operations.
- Supplier: the named independent hotel or property operator remains responsible for delivering the stay.
- Payments: live collection must remain disabled until the contracted provider, disclosures, reconciliation, refunds, chargebacks, and settlement controls are certified.
- Transport: cars, buses, and flights remain marked coming soon and are outside the hotel-first launch.

## Management compliance decisions

1. Confirm whether Mandyal Travels is a marketplace e-commerce entity, booking facilitator, agent, or another category for each enabled hotel workflow.
2. Approve the description of the contracting hotel, payment recipient, platform role, and allocation of responsibility before booking and in the confirmation.
3. Approve all customer terms, limitations, mandatory consumer remedies, governing law, dispute language, and proposed contractual venue.
4. Approve the cancellation deadline, timezone, deductions, no-show treatment, refund eligibility, refund initiation target, provider processing estimate, chargeback treatment, and supplier set-off language.
5. Approve the privacy notice, processing purposes, consent design, retention schedule, processors, breach process, child-data position, rights handling, and phased DPDP compliance plan.
6. Confirm required seller disclosures, grievance workflow, complaint acknowledgement and resolution service levels, and records to retain.
7. Approve the hotel partner agreement and electronic acceptance evidence, including commissions, taxes, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, safety, data protection, suspension, termination, audit, and proportionate indemnity.
8. Confirm that transport remains unavailable and that no hotel-first content creates or implies a motor-vehicle aggregator service.
9. Confirm applicable hotel, tourism, local-body, fire, guest-registration, identity, insurance, accessibility, and property-level obligations for partner onboarding.
10. Confirm the wording and timing for publication after GST and payment-provider status are known.
