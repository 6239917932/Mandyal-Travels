# Legal launch readiness

The public policy center contains operational drafts, not approved legal instruments. Partner
applications and public supplier writes remain disabled for the Cashfree review launch. Do not
enable commercial hotel or vehicle listings, booking, or payment until the decisions and evidence
below are complete.

## Business identity and grievance disclosure

- Confirm the exact legal entity or proprietor name and trading name.
- Publish the complete principal and registered postal addresses.
- Appoint and publish the Mandyal Travels grievance officer's name, designation, phone number, and
  email address.
- Configure a complaint workflow that acknowledges applicable e-commerce complaints within 48
  hours and resolves them within one month.
- Require every marketplace supplier to publish its legal/business name, address, customer contact,
  and grievance contact and designation.

## Booking model

- Decide per product whether Mandyal Travels is only a supplier-direct referral channel or
  facilitates the booking or payment.
- Display that role, the named contracting supplier, the payment recipient, and the supplier's
  cancellation terms before commitment and on the confirmation.
- Never use a referral label if the actual workflow controls fare, booking, payment, driver
  allocation, or transport fulfilment in a manner that may make Mandyal Travels an aggregator.

## Partner agreement and onboarding

- Obtain Indian counsel approval for separate hotel and vehicle partner agreements.
- Require versioned electronic acceptance before activation and preserve the accepted version,
  timestamp, signer authority, and audit evidence.
- Complete KYC, ownership/authority, tax, bank, licence, permit, insurance, safety, and expiry
  reviews appropriate to the supplier and State.
- Contractually allocate service delivery, customer incidents, refunds, chargebacks, settlement
  set-off, taxes, data protection, audit, suspension, termination, and proportionate indemnity.

## Vehicle service classification

- Obtain a State-specific legal opinion on whether the proposed car workflow is a motor-vehicle
  aggregator service under section 93 of the Motor Vehicles Act and the Motor Vehicle Aggregator
  Guidelines, 2025.
- If it is, obtain the required licence and implement passenger insurance, driver and vehicle
  controls, safety features, grievance handling, and all Central and State requirements before
  launch.

## Payments, cancellations, and refunds

- Approve and publish customer-visible review and refund-initiation timelines.
- Disclose the total price, taxes, every fee, refundability, cancellation deadline and timezone,
  deductions, and no-show terms before payment.
- Ensure supplier settlement remains on hold while a linked refund or material dispute is open.
- Test original-method refunds, provider failures, duplicate payment, supplier cancellation,
  chargeback, and reconciliation paths with Cashfree before live processing.

## Privacy and cookies

- Complete the personal-data inventory, purpose, sharing, retention, processor, security, breach,
  child-data, and rights-response controls required for the actual service.
- Publish the contact responsible for data-processing questions and the applicable statutory
  escalation route.
- Keep optional analytics and advertising cookies disabled until the provider, purpose, retention,
  recipient, and consent controls are approved and disclosed.

## Legal review record

- Have qualified Indian counsel review the public policies, customer booking flow, partner
  agreements, State transport position, tax and payment flow, insurance, and jurisdiction wording.
- Replace every `pending-legal-approval` or `pending-commercial-approval` version only after signed
  approval, then preserve the approved text and effective date.

## Primary regulatory sources used for the operational draft

- Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and 2021 amendment.
- Central Consumer Protection Authority advisory on marketplace seller information.
- Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023.
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025,
  including their phased commencement.
- Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines, 2025 and applicable State requirements.

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice.
