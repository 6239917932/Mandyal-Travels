# Bus aggregator activation

The portal has governed operator routes, dated trips, fares, seat capacity, refundable rules, distribution controls, customer search, exact seat validation, passenger capture, and booking records. A live aggregator adapter must translate its inventory into these normalized contracts without bypassing domain validation.

Activation requires commercial approval, sandbox credentials, HTTPS host allow-listing, route/location mapping, stable trip and seat identifiers, idempotent booking and cancellation, signed callbacks, retry/reconciliation procedures, settlement reporting, and monitored pilot traffic. Fixtures and direct operator inventory remain clearly identified until a provider is certified.
