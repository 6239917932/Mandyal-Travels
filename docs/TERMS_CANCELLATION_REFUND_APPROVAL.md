# Terms, cancellation, and refund legal approval

The booking engines preserve quoted terms, rate-level cancellation rules, structured refund cutoffs, property timezones, payment/refund records, and customer-visible non-guarantee wording. Legal and commercial owners must approve the customer terms, supplier terms, cancellation/no-show rules, refund timelines, convenience fees, force-majeure treatment, dispute resolution, chargebacks, service failure remedies, and product-specific liabilities.

Published policies must be versioned and bound to each booking. A later policy edit must never retroactively alter the terms accepted for an existing order.
