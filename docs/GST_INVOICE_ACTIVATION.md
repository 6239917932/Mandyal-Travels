# GST and invoice activation

The portal records immutable price components, currencies, captured payment totals, refunds, supplier tax details, and downloadable booking invoices. Production tax calculation must be driven by approved tax configuration and the legally correct supplier/customer/place-of-supply context.

Before launch, a qualified tax adviser must approve GST registrations, SAC/HSN treatment, intra/inter-state rules, CGST/SGST/IGST splits, TCS/TDS obligations, credit notes, cancellation adjustments, invoice numbering, retention, e-invoicing applicability, reconciliation, and accounting exports. The current invoice must remain marked provisional wherever required data or approval is absent.
