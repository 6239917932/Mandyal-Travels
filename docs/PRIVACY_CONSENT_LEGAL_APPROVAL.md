# Privacy and consent legal approval

The portal implements versioned consent records, withdrawal, data-access/correction/deletion requests, retention governance, scoped authentication, and audit trails. Legal counsel must approve the privacy notice, cookie notice, consent purposes, lawful bases, child/minor treatment, processor list, international transfers, retention periods, grievance contacts, and jurisdiction-specific rights before production publication.

Consent must remain granular and revocable. Necessary processing cannot be bundled with marketing permission, and withdrawals must propagate to messaging and profiling systems.
